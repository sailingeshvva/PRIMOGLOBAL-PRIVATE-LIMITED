How Primoglobal Works

A trade workflow international buyers can understand before the first call.

  1. 01Understand Buyer Requirement

    Product, specification, quantity, packaging, destination and purchase frequency are clarified first.

  2. 02Source From Verified Suppliers

    Relevant Indian suppliers are identified and reviewed for the requested requirement.

  3. 03Quality & Specification Review

    Specifications, sample expectations and quality checks are aligned before commercial discussion.

  4. 04Commercial Quotation

    Pricing, terms and practical shipment considerations are shared transparently.

  5. 05Order & Documentation

    Order details and required trade documents are coordinated with discipline.

  6. 06Logistics & Shipment

    Shipment requirements are planned with supplier, buyer and logistics partners.

  7. 07Delivery & Long-Term Partnership

    The goal is dependable execution and repeatable commercial relationships.
